Alpha Bot – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13 Mei 2026

Collected information is used solely to provide task management and automation functionality within Discord servers.

Server administrators may request deletion of stored data related to their server.

6. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance of changes.
